Health Services

Comprehensive healthcare for students at Aditiya University

Clinic Hours & Location

The university health clinic is located in Building A - Room 105. Services are available Monday through Friday:

  • 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM
  • 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM

For emergencies outside clinic hours, please contact the campus emergency line or visit the nearest hospital.

Services Offered

  • General medical consultations and treatment
  • Mental health counseling and support
  • Vaccinations and immunizations
  • Health screenings and wellness programs
  • Referrals to specialized healthcare providers
