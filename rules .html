Aditiya University - Rules and Regulations

General Conduct

  • Students are expected to maintain professionalism and respect towards faculty, staff, and peers.
  • Disruptive behavior and harassment are strictly prohibited.
  • Compliance with all campus safety rules and emergency procedures is mandatory.

Academic Integrity

  • Plagiarism, cheating, and any form of academic dishonesty will result in disciplinary action.
  • Students must submit their own original work unless collaboration is explicitly permitted.
  • Examinations and assessments must be taken under the conditions set by the institution without unauthorized assistance.

Attendance

  • Regular attendance is required for all courses.
  • Students must notify instructors and/or administration about absences in advance wherever possible.
  • Excessive absences may lead to loss of credit for a course.

Use of Facilities

  • Students must respect and properly use all college property and facilities.
  • Unauthorized access to restricted areas is prohibited.
  • Use of labs and libraries must comply with posted rules and schedules.

Dress Code

  • Students should dress appropriately in a manner respectful of the learning environment.
  • Clothing with offensive or inappropriate language or graphics is not allowed.

Disciplinary Procedures

  • Violations of rules and regulations may result in warnings, probation, suspension, or expulsion.
  • Students have the right to appeal disciplinary decisions following the college’s appeal process.
  • All students are encouraged to familiarize themselves with detailed college policies available in the student handbook.
